The course

Seven modules.
Nothing skipped, nothing wasted.

Around 30 minutes each, self-paced, with scenario-based questions rather than tick-box quizzes — and content written for a reading age of 9, so it lands with every student, not just confident readers.

  1. 01

    Cyberbullying & child-on-child abuse

    What it is, why it hurts, and exactly what to do — including image-based abuse and upskirting, named plainly.

  2. 02

    Passwords & account security

    Building a password that actually holds up, and spotting the messages designed to talk it out of you.

  3. 03

    Privacy & personal data

    Digital footprints, age restrictions, and the rights every student already has over their own data.

  4. 04

    Social media safety

    Fake profiles, grooming red flags, and the pressure of online challenges — covered directly, not hinted at.

  5. 05

    Phishing & online scams

    Real examples of scam texts and emails, plus how to spot the apps designed to keep them hooked.

  6. 06

    Misinformation & critical thinking

    A simple method for checking what's true online, before it gets shared any further.

  7. 07

    Wellbeing & screen time

    The comparison trap, healthy habits, and why some scrolling feels worse than others.
