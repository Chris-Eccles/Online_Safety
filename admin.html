Lessons

Pick a year and a group, then tap a lesson to switch it on or off for that group. Students see the change next time they open or refresh the page.

Class results

How the group answered each quiz question: do now, hinge and exit ticket. Anonymous: one answer per device (the first one), no names. It updates every 10 seconds while this page is open.

Homework

Years 10 to 13. Upload a homework .txt (the hw-homework-builder format), check the preview, publish, then copy the link into Teams. Students can only reach a homework through its link. Each hand-in is marked and emailed to you with a CSV and a JSON file; nothing about the student is kept on the website.

Showing a colleague

Demo as a student (top bar) opens the student site as a hidden demo group, Year 7 "DEMO", with every Level Up and Pitch It! lesson switched on. No passwords to type, it doesn't appear in the students' group list, and its quiz answers are left out of whole-year class results. Press Sign out on the student page to leave the demo.

To show homework, use Preview on any homework in the Homework tab: it shows exactly what students see, with hand-in switched off.
