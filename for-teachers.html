For teachers

Everything you need.
Nothing you have to manage.

Here's exactly what happens once you've bought seats, with no guessing and no surprise admin.

🎨

Personalise it in minutes

Add your school's name, logo, and your Designated Safeguarding Lead's name and location. Every student sees exactly who to go to if something worries them, by name and not just by role.

🔑

One code per student

Generate a code for every seat you've bought. Hand them out however suits your lesson: printed, projected, or shared through Teams or Google Classroom.

📊

Results, straight to your inbox

Once a student finishes, you get their pass/fail result, quiz score, and full written reflections. That's enough for your own safeguarding records, without chasing anyone for it.

🛡️

Built to be inspected

Every module is mapped to KCSIE, RSE statutory guidance, and Education for a Connected World. See the full curriculum mapping.

📊
Track it all from your dashboard: every teacher gets a personal dashboard link in their welcome email. You can see seat usage at a glance and each student’s progress through the modules, and download finished certificates as soon as they’re earned.
