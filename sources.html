Sources

Sources and references

We only use statistics we can trace to a published source, and we show you where they come from. Numbers change when new research is published, so we re-check every link each month and review each figure at least once a year.

If a link has moved, or you cannot find a figure, search for the publisher and report title. It is a good habit to look up a statistic for yourself before you share it, and it is exactly what the course teaches.

The law, guidance and where to get help

  • Safeguarding guidance for schools: Keeping children safe in education, Department for Education
  • Platforms' legal duties to protect children: Online Safety Act 2023, legislation.gov.uk
  • Upskirting is a criminal offence: Voyeurism (Offences) Act 2019, legislation.gov.uk
  • Support for children: Childline, NSPCC
  • Getting a nude image taken down: Report Remove, Childline and the Internet Watch Foundation
  • Reporting online grooming: CEOP Safety Centre, National Crime Agency
  • Reporting scams and phishing: Phishing and scam reporting, National Cyber Security Centre
  • Reporting fraud: Action Fraud, City of London Police
